## Why This Blog?

Data engineering is mostly invisible work. Nobody sees the pipelines, the schema decisions, or the late-night incident that almost broke the dashboard. I created DataSubway to change that — to make the underground machinery of data visible, approachable, and worth talking about. The subway metaphor is intentional: every great city runs on infrastructure nobody thinks about until it stops working. I build that infrastructure for data, and this is where I write about it.
