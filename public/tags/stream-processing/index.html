The Great Train Debate: Batch vs. Stream Processing

In the Invisible City, not every train travels at the same speed. Some are massive freight trains that move heavy loads once a night, while others are sleek, light subway cars that zip through the tunnels every single second. Choosing how to move your data is one of the most important decisions a Conductor can make. If you send a “Real-Time” message on a “Midnight Freight” train, the city won’t react in time.
